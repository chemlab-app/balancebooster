📖 Redox Balancing Guide (Acidic Medium)

Balancing redox reactions by the half-equation method splits a reaction into reduction and oxidation reactions, which are balanced individually and then combined. In an acidic medium, follow these precise step-by-step rules:

1 Atoms First

Balance atoms other than Hydrogen and Oxygen first by multiplying using standard stoichiometric coefficients.

2 Oxygen (O)

Balance oxygen atoms by adding H₂O molecules to the side deficient in oxygen.

3 Hydrogen (H)

Balance hydrogen atoms by adding hydrogen ions (H⁺) to the deficient side.

4 Charge Balance

Balance net charge by adding electrons (e⁻) to the more positive side.

💡 PRO TIP FOR COMBINING HALF-EQUATIONS:
Before adding half-equations, make sure electrons (e⁻) in both equations are equal. If not, multiply each entire equation by coefficients to synchronize electron counts, and cancel out identical species!
